Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Neon Stack Master (“we”, “our”, or “the Game”) is a free browser-based game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Local Storage data — High score, unlocked skins, daily streak and last play date are stored in your browser on your device. The game code does not send this gameplay data to its operator. Browser extensions or other services on your device are outside our control.
  • No account required — We do not ask for name, email, phone number or any personal information.
  • Advertising — No third-party advertising script is included in this project version. If advertising is added later, this policy will be updated to identify the active provider and explain relevant cookies, device information, consent choices and opt-out options.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress on the same device
  • To operate the game and retain your locally saved game progress
  • To improve the game experience

3. Third-Party Services

This project version does not intentionally load an advertising partner. If a third-party advertising service is enabled in a future release, that provider may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies under its own policy. We will update this page and implement any consent controls required for the audiences and regions served before enabling it.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Storage & Security

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Clearing your browser data will reset your high score and unlocked items.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/skamaldev/neon-stack-master
