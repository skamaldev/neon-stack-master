Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Neon Stack Master (“we”, “our”, or “the Game”) is a free browser-based game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Local Storage data — High score, unlocked skins, daily streak and last play date are stored only on your device using the browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • No account required — We do not ask for name, email, phone number or any personal information.
  • Advertising — We use third-party advertising networks (such as Adsterra) to display ads. These networks may collect device and usage data according to their own privacy policies in order to show relevant advertisements.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress on the same device
  • To display advertisements that help keep the game free
  • To improve the game experience

3. Third-Party Services

Advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies. We do not control these third-party practices. Please review the privacy policy of any ad network used on the site.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Storage & Security

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Clearing your browser data will reset your high score and unlocked items.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the contact method provided on the website (if available) or by opening an issue on the project repository.
