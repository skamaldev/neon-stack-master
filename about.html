How to Play

Neon Stack Master – Free browser stack game

Basic Controls

  • Tap / Click / Space — Drop the moving neon block
  • Stack blocks as high as you can without missing
  • The more precise your placement, the better your score

Perfect & Combo

Land the block almost exactly on top of the previous one → PERFECT.

Consecutive Perfects build a Combo. Higher combos = much higher score.

Fever Mode 🔥

Get 6 Perfects in a row to enter Fever Mode.

  • Score is doubled
  • Blocks get special golden glow
  • Lasts for a limited number of drops
  • Missing a Perfect ends Fever Mode early

Daily Challenge

Every day has a special seeded challenge. Same sequence for all players that day. Compete with friends using the Share Score button.

Skins

Unlock new neon color themes by reaching higher scores. Your progress is saved automatically on this device.

Continue

After Game Over, the game may offer one free continuation per run. This version does not claim that a rewarded advertisement is being shown.

Tips for High Scores

  • Focus on rhythm — don’t rush when speed increases
  • Prioritize Perfects to trigger Fever Mode
  • Play Daily Challenge every day to build streak
  • Share your score to challenge friends
